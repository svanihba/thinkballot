How to vote by November 3

  • Not registered yet? You can still register and vote the same day, through November 3, at your county elections office or any in-person voting location in your county. Same Day registration · Check your registration
  • Voting by mail? Your ballot must be postmarked on or before November 3, or dropped off at a ballot drop-off location by 8:00 p.m. on November 3. If you mail it on November 3, ask for a postmark inside a post office. Track your ballot
  • Voting in person? Early voting locations are open now in many counties. On November 3, polls are open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Find a voting location
  • Questions? Contact your county elections office
